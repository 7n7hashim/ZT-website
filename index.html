Our story

Born at a window seat.Built over 14 years.

Zaras Travel was founded by Fatma Mohamed after a career with Kenya Airways, on one belief: travel should feel personal, not processed.

Effortless. Personal. Every journey. Fatma Mohamed, Founder
The sky

Years above the clouds

Fatma spent her career with Kenya Airways learning how the world moves, and how travellers feel when it moves smoothly.

The spark

A conviction at 38,000 feet

Between departures and arrivals, one belief kept returning: travel should feel personal. Not processed.

The founding

Zaras Travel is born

She brought aviation’s discipline down to earth: a travel company that answers with a name, not a queue.

Today

14+years

Of trusted journeys, from family safaris to corporate itineraries. The promise hasn’t changed: your plans, handled like they’re ours.
